## Timeline

- 1962 [Seattle World's Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Century_21_Exposition) led to a spike in urban developement and motels that were then underutilized after the Fair
    - Led to increased sex work (from the street to the motels)
- 2018 CESTA/FOSTA passed
    - Backpages, Craig's List personal ads, etc shut down
    - Some motels closed
- 2020 Seattle's loitering law was repealed (to reduce targeting unhoused neighbors)
- 2023 Multiple shots around Aurora and 101st and nearby blocks. Many neighbors banded together.
    - Led to SOAP law
    - Increased SPD emphasis patrols and beat cops
    - Aurora and 101st/107th was blocked (on the west side of Aurora) by SDOT under the direction of the deputy mayor (the same morning footage of 3 shootings within 2 weeks was sent to local media and many Seattle city officials)
        - SDOT planned to block off other streets and later backed out of that plan
- 2023 [IsJonAJohn.com](https://isjonajohn.com/faq/) is created
- 2024-09-24 Seattle [SOAP law](soap-law) is passed
- 2026 Spike in gunshots around Aurora and 98th
    - Anonymous neighbors in the area built planter boxes and blocked Aurora and 97th/98th/102nd
    - A few days later, SDOT replaced planter boxes on 97th/102nd with (jersey barrier) chicaines
        - 98th planter boxes haven't been removed (as of June 5th, 2026)

## FAQs / Misconceptions

### "It's always been like this"

Street sex work on Aurora has been around for decades but (some combination of) CESTA/FOSTA, Seattle's loitering law being repealed, and motels closing led to increased sex trafficking. Increased trafficking led to gun violence as pimps fight for territory.

### "All trafficking is bad"

All human trafficking **is** bad. However, the legal definition would also label a sex worker (or friend, etc) that's helping another sex worker escape from a bad situation across state lines a human trafficker!

### Street sex workers used be Seattle locals

This is based on conversations with a neighbor that lived just off Aurora and used to know and help a few local women that worked on Aurora. They also helped provide shelter/a place to hide and food to some (newer) women who were not local. This neighbor claims that most of the local sex workers have slowly been pushed out (or in some cases recruited) by out-of-town pimps.

## Definitions

Theses are the definitions used on this site. They differ from normal definitions.

- pimp: someone who recruits street sex workers, employing violence and manipulation. Not to be confused with a body guard.
- prostitute: a sex worker who advertises on the street
