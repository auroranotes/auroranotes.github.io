# Stay Out of Area of Prostitution Law

Full text of law that was passed: [CB 120836 v3](https://seattle.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=13321960&GUID=DA89CBD4-E397-4E5E-8349-248BE7A2F4EA)

Full text of originally proposed law: [CB 120836 v1](https://seattle.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=13209997&GUID=10D5FFA7-12C7-4E47-A812-D0ACEFBF852D)

Many neighbors along Aurora between 90th and 110th supported the bill at public comment. Many sex worker related advocacy groups did NOT suppor the bill at public comment.
